Cepta is building an efficient payment infrastructure for small businesses and large enterprises. Whether your goal is to receive card payments, explore payout options to local banks, or accept funds via transfer, Cepta is for you! This documentation is made to offer a seamless integration experience for developers who are looking to explore the Cepta API. Cepta supports payments from Mastercard, Visa, and Verve, ensuring broad accessibility for businesses and customers alike.
